**Step 2: Fix the orphaned body matching in serialize.rs.**

The root cause: `finalize()` sorts MethodDefs by `parent.map(|p| p.0).unwrap_or(usize::MAX)`. After sorting, orphaned MethodDefs (those with `def_id == None`) appear in a different relative order than the bodies were emitted in `body/mod.rs` (which emits reflectable bodies first, then lambda bodies).

The fix must ensure orphaned MethodDefs and orphaned bodies are matched by a stable key, not by positional cursor. Two approaches (choose whichever is cleanest):

**Approach A (preferred): Tag orphaned bodies and MethodDefs with a matching index.**
In `body/mod.rs`, when emitting orphaned bodies, set a new field `orphan_tag: Option<u32>` on `EmittedBody` that encodes a unique sequence number. In `module_builder.rs`, when adding orphaned MethodDefs (via `add_lambda_method_def` or `add_reflectable_method_def`), assign the same sequence number. In `serialize.rs`, match by `orphan_tag` instead of positional cursor.

**Approach B (simpler): Sort orphaned body indices to match the MethodDef order.**
After `finalize()` sorts MethodDefs, the orphaned MethodDefs appear in a specific order. Collect the orphan tags/categories (reflectable vs lambda) from the MethodDef table post-sort, then reorder `orphaned_body_indices` to match. Concretely: in `serialize.rs`, instead of collecting orphaned bodies in emission order, collect them grouped by category (reflectable first, then lambda) and within each group maintain emission order. Then, when iterating `finalized_method_def_entries()`, the cursor-based matching works because both sides use the same ordering.

**Key constraint:** The fix must not change the Module binary format or the MethodDef table layout — only the mapping from MethodDefs to bodies during serialization.
